This paper describes coherent super decay, an effect in which several damped oscillators combined dissipate energy much faster than any one of them alone. The oscillator parameters are obtained by response function decomposition, which approximates a fast-decaying reference resonator using several slower-decaying ones. Experiments and finite element simulations show that tuned mass dampers with a total mass below 0.2% of that of a stainless steel plate can raise the damping ratio of the first resonance of the plate to 4.6%.

I carried out this work as an undergraduate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I developed Fast Fourier Transform analysis scripts in MATLAB to isolate and test the eigenfrequencies of prototype dampers. A [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.08621) is available on arXiv.
