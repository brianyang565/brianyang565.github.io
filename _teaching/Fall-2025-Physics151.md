I served as a Graduate Student Instructor (GSI) for the first course in the introductory Physics for the Life Sciences Laboratory sequence. The experiments cover reaction time, motion, gravity and falling bodies, Newton's laws, static equilibrium, a biomechanical model of the forearm, hydrostatic weighing, fluid mechanics and flow, the measurement of blood pressure, and food calorimetry. I guided students through the experiments and basic instrumentation, troubleshot the laboratory hardware during sessions, and introduced students to statistical uncertainty, error propagation, and clear technical reporting.
