I served as a Graduate Student Instructor (GSI) for the second course in the introductory Physics for the Life Sciences Laboratory sequence, which covers electricity and magnetism, including light and wave phenomena. I guided students through the experiments and basic instrumentation, troubleshot the laboratory hardware during sessions, and introduced students to statistical uncertainty, error propagation, and clear technical reporting.
