I have served as a Graduate Student Instructor (GSI) for this laboratory in most fall and winter semesters. The experiments cover reaction time, motion, gravity and falling bodies, Newton's laws, static equilibrium, a biomechanical model of the forearm, hydrostatic weighing, fluid mechanics and flow, the measurement of blood pressure, and food calorimetry. I guide students through the experiments and basic instrumentation, troubleshoot the laboratory hardware during sessions, and introduce students to statistical uncertainty, error propagation, and clear technical reporting.
