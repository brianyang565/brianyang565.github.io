I am a Ph.D. candidate in experimental atomic, molecular, and optical physics at the University of Michigan, where I work with Prof. Alex Kuzmich in the [Kuzmich Research Group](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/kuzmich-lab/). My research concerns collective light–matter interaction in ultracold ensembles of <sup>87</sup>Rb atoms. We prepare coherences between the ground state and highly excited Rydberg states, and we study the directional, phase-matched light that the ensemble emits when those coherences are read out.

Research
======

In [Dipole Moment of a Superatom](/publication/2024-dipole-moment-of-a-superatom) (*Phys. Rev. Lett.*, 2024), we used homodyne detection to measure the collective dipole moment of an ensemble prepared in a superposition of spatially phased Dicke states having at most two excitations. Depending on whether the interference between the atomic field and the reference field was constructive or destructive, the combined field exhibited super-Poisson or sub-Poisson statistics.

In [Superradiant quantum beats driven by ground-state–Rydberg-state coherences](/publication/2025-superradiant-quantum-beats) (*Phys. Rev. A*, 2025), we observed quantum beats in the phase-matched emission of a cold atomic ensemble. Both the time-resolved and the time-integrated beating patterns depend on the storage time between the excitation and readout pulses, and a detailed model of the readout and emission processes reproduces the observed signatures quantitatively.

Building the apparatus
======

I particularly enjoy building experimental systems and seeing them work. In the lab, I have built laser intensity servos and acousto-optic modulator and fiber paths, upgraded the timing control of our experiment to the Cicero/Atticus framework, and characterized the phase noise of our 780 nm laser. I also write standard operating procedures for our lab devices so that these skills pass on to junior group members, and I currently coach undergraduates working on a two-axis acousto-optic deflector test bench for atom rearrangement. As a side project, I also wrote a [simulation of a Pound–Drever–Hall laser lock](/portfolio/pdh-lock-simulation/) based on the parameters of our experiment.

Background
======

I received my B.S. in Honors Physics from the University of Michigan in 2021. My honors thesis analyzed an iterative beam-shaping algorithm for simultaneous amplitude and phase control. I also carried out summer research at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I worked on mechanical vibration damping in 2017 and on an electromagnet for a magneto-optical trap in 2018.
