Quantum beats in superradiance arise from interference between collective emission from independent transitions, and they differ in this respect from single-atom quantum beats. We observe such beats from ground-state–Rydberg-state coherences in the phase-matched emission of a cold atomic ensemble. Both the time-resolved and the time-integrated beating patterns depend on the storage time between the excitation and readout pulses, and a detailed model of the readout and emission processes quantitatively reproduces the observed signatures.

The results were published as [Superradiant quantum beats driven by ground-state–Rydberg-state coherences](/publication/2025-superradiant-quantum-beats). The abstract is available in the [meeting archive](https://meetings-archive.aps.org/damop/2026/f04/4/).
