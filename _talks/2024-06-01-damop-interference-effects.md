We have studied the phase-matched output of a strongly interacting atomic ensemble by homodyne detection with the excitation laser field. Depending on whether the interference between the atomic and laser fields is constructive or destructive, either super-Poisson or sub-Poisson statistics is observed.

The results were published as [Dipole Moment of a Superatom](/publication/2024-dipole-moment-of-a-superatom). The talk is listed in the [session archive](https://meetings-archive.aps.org/damop/2024/g09) of the meeting.
